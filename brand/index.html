● Colour

One ink

● Usage

Keep it clean

  • Clear space. Keep at least the height of the O's counter clear around the logo on every side.
  • Minimum size. Wordmark 24 px (8 mm) wide on screen, 18 mm in print.
  • One colour at a time. Signal on paper is the default; ink with signal water, or paper on signal and night, are the alternatives.
  • Never stretch, outline, rotate, add effects to, or re-set the logo in another font, and never move, flatten or drop the waterline.
  • Name in text as “Olaz AI”: capital O, never “OLAZ AI” in running text or “Olaz A.I.”. The logo sets OLAZ in capitals.
  • Voice. Plain, direct and specific: what we build and who it helps, never buzzwords.
