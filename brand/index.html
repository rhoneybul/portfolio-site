Logo, colour and type for Fohm AI: technical leadership and AI engineering by Rob Honeybul. The name is foam, respelled: the white water a wave leaves behind, and it sits at the waterline.

● Logo

The mark

The wordmark is FOHM set in DM Serif Display at the waterline: above a gentle wave the letters are solid, below it they sit in the water as a 30% tint of the same colour. The lockups add AI in Inter Tight. Use the stacked logo where there is room, the horizontal one in narrow bars, and the wordmark alone once the name is already clear.

The symbol is a round of sea at the waterline: solid water below a rising swell, three bubbles lifting off it. It stands in for the name where there is no room for it, and sits beside the wordmark in the lockup.

● Colour

One ink

Signal orange carries the brand. Use it for the mark, headings and labels; keep body copy in ink. Paper is the page. There is no dark version: the brand lives on light.

● Usage

Keep it clean

  • Clear space. Keep at least the height of the O's counter clear around the logo on every side.
  • Minimum size. Wordmark 24 px (8 mm) wide on screen, 18 mm in print.
  • One colour at a time. Signal on paper is the default; ink with signal water, or paper on signal, are the alternatives.
  • Never stretch, outline, rotate, add effects to, or re-set the logo in another font, and never move, flatten or drop the waterline.
  • Name in text as “Fohm AI”: capital O, never “FOHM AI” in running text or “Fohm A.I.”. The logo sets FOHM in capitals.
  • Voice. Plain, direct and specific: what we build and who it helps, never buzzwords.
