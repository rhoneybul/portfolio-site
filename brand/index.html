● Usage

Keep it clean

  • Clear space. Keep at least the height of the O's counter clear around the logo on every side.
  • Minimum size. Wordmark 64 px (18 mm) wide; below that, use the icon.
  • One colour at a time. Signal on paper is the default; ink with a signal dot, or paper on signal and night, are the alternatives.
  • Never stretch, outline, rotate, add effects to, or re-set the logo in another font, and never move or drop the charge dot.
  • Name in text as “ION Consulting”: ION in capitals, never “Ion” or “I.O.N.”.
  • Voice. Plain, direct and specific: what we build and who it helps, never buzzwords.
